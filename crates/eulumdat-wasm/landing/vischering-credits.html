Burg Vischering scene – credits

The Burg Vischering scene of the Skyglow demo (Lüdinghausen, Germany) is built from open data of Geobasis NRW: the 3D-Mesh 2024, the 3D measurement data (LiDAR) 2024 and the LoD2 building models, licensed under Datenlizenz Deutschland – Zero – 2.0.

The castle walls are textured with photographs projected onto the LoD2 building model. These façade textures are a derivative of the photos below and are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0; each photo keeps its own licence. Roof, paving and timber use CC0 materials from Poly Haven.
